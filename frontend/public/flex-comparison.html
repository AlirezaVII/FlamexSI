How to interpret the comparison

Across 258 usable experiments, higher oxygen ranked with higher droplet burning rate. Held-out Spearman correlations were 0.571 for the primary split and 0.703 for the date split. Within-fuel descriptive correlations were 0.687 for methanol and 0.432 for heptane. Pressure, diluent and droplet size can differ between groups.

Use this comparison to select conditions for a follow-up experiment on fire prevention. Cabin oxygen limits and extinguishing thresholds require separate evidence.
